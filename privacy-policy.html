Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information Kavoir Systems ("we", "us") collects through the Energy Operating Score quiz at this site, how it is used, and the choices you have. By submitting the form on this site, you agree to the practices described here.

What we collect

When you complete the quiz and submit the email capture form, we collect:

  • The email address you provide.
  • Your consent status (whether you ticked the consent checkbox) and the timestamp of your submission.
  • Your quiz answers and the resulting Energy Operating Score, used only to generate the report shown to you.

We also use standard website analytics tools (such as Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity) to understand how visitors use this site. These tools may collect general technical data like your browser type, device, approximate location, and on-page behavior. This data is aggregated and is not used to personally identify you.

How we use your information

  • To send you the full protocol / results email you unlocked by submitting the form.
  • To send occasional related emails, such as follow-up content or offers connected to the Energy Operating Score.
  • To improve this site and the quiz experience based on aggregate usage patterns.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Where your information is stored

Your email address and consent status are stored in a private Google Sheet ("Kavoir Subscribers"), submitted via a secure Google Apps Script endpoint at the moment you complete the form. Access to this sheet is restricted to Kavoir Systems.

Legal basis and consent

We only add you to our subscriber list if you explicitly tick the consent checkbox before submitting the form. You are free to decline, in which case your results will not be unlocked and no email address is collected. Ticking the box is your affirmative, informed consent to receive the result email and related communications.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw your consent and unsubscribe at any time, using the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or by contacting us directly.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data.

Data retention

We retain your email address and consent record for as long as you remain subscribed, or as needed to comply with legal obligations. If you unsubscribe or request deletion, we will remove your data from our active subscriber list within a reasonable time.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses cookies and similar technologies through third-party analytics providers to understand aggregate site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them will not affect your ability to take the quiz.

Third-party services

We rely on the following categories of third-party service providers to operate this site:

  • Google Workspace / Google Sheets — to receive and store subscriber submissions.
  • Website analytics providers — to measure site performance and usage.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy and security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise your data rights, contact us at thekavoir@proton.me.
